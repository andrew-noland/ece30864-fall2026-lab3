Pros:
1. Agile allows teams to adapt quickly when requirements or customer needs change during development.
2. Agile provides frequent feedback through short development cycles, allowing problems to be found and corrected earlier

Cons:
1. Since requirements can frequently change, it can be harder to predict the exact schedule, cost, and final scope of a project.
2. Agile provides frequent communication and participation from team members and stakeholders, which can requie significant time and coordination
